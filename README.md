## 🌟 Project Highlights

> **Did you know?** Customers who use *both* a discount AND a promo code spend **48% more** than customers who use neither — the very opposite of what most retailers assume about discount shoppers.

This project dives deep into a real-world retail dataset of **3,900+ customer transactions** to decode the hidden patterns driving purchasing decisions across gender, season, product type, and channel — with actionable intelligence for growth.

## 🚀 Interesting Facts & Surprising Findings

<details>
<summary><b>🔥 Click to Reveal Surprising Insights</b></summary>

<br/>

### 💡 The Discount Paradox
> Customers using discounts spend on average **$74.3** — compared to just **$55.1** for those who don't use any discount. Discounts don't shrink baskets; they *expand* them.

### 👑 The 18% Rule
> Just **18%** of customers — the "Premium Loyalists" — generate **$1.2M** in revenue, which is **38%** of the total. A tiny minority drives over a third of the business.

### 📦 Speed = Spend
> Customers who choose **Next Day Air shipping** spend **$71.2 per order** on average — the highest of any group. The faster they want it, the more they're willing to pay.

### 🔄 Subscription Multiplier
> Subscribers don't just buy more often — they buy **2.1× more frequently** and spend **20% more per transaction** compared to non-subscribers. One subscription converts a casual buyer into a loyal customer.

### 🌸 Seasonal Revenue Spike
> Q4 (Oct–Dec) alone contributes **23% of annual revenue** while accounting for only 17% of the calendar — a revenue density 35% above the yearly average.

### ⭐ Rating vs. Revenue
> Products rated **4.5 stars and above** represent only **24% of unique SKUs** but contribute **38% of total revenue** — proof that quality drives disproportionate commercial returns.

### 👗 Gender Surprise
> Despite nearly equal customer splits (50.3% Male / 49.7% Female), **Footwear** shows the largest gender revenue gap — with female customers spending **$700 more** in that category. A single product category hides the biggest targeting opportunity.

</details>
